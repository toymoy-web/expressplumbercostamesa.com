Before We Arrive in Newport Beach: Steps for a Burst Pipe or Active Leak

Close the main valve.

Most Newport Beach homes have the house shutoff where the supply line enters, often on the wall facing the street. On Balboa Island and the Peninsula, check near the front walk or a garage wall box. Turn it clockwise until it stops.

Cut power to wet areas.

Switch off breakers for any room where water is reaching outlets or appliances, as long as the panel itself is dry. Garage water heaters and laundry closets are the usual trouble spots.

Clear the path of the water.

Move rugs, electronics and stored boxes away from the leak. In low bayfront homes on slab floors, water spreads fast, so start at the edges of the wet area.

Pumped sewers on the islands and the Peninsula

Much of Newport Beach sits low, close to the bay. The city's sewer system relies on 21 pump stations to lift wastewater from lower areas and the island communities into the regional trunk lines. Regional pump stations such as Lido and 14th Street also serve the Peninsula.

That matters when a backup happens. If several homes on a street back up at once, the problem may be in the public system, and the agency should be called. If it is only your home, the blockage is usually in your own lateral, and a camera run shows where.
