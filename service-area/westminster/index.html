A sewer district older than the city

Sewer service in Westminster comes from the Midway City Sanitary District, which formed in 1939, years before the city incorporated in 1957. The district opened bids for its first sewer mains in 1953 and now maintains more than 170 miles of sewer pipe for Westminster and Midway City. It is also one of the few agencies in Orange County that still collects trash with its own crews.

The practical point: if sewage is spilling from a manhole or the street main, that is the district's to handle, and its office line is (714) 893-3553. If the backup is only in your home, the blockage is almost always in your own lateral, and that is our job.

Many Westminster laterals were connected to mains laid in the 1950s. Pipe of that age is a common place for roots and offset joints.

Water pressure in older Westminster homes

Low pressure throughout the house is one of the most common Westminster complaints. In homes of this age, galvanized steel supply pipe narrows from the inside as rust builds up, and the effect is gradual, so owners often notice it only when a shower and a washing machine run at the same time.

The other cause is a failing pressure reducing valve at the main line. We test with a gauge at a hose bib before recommending anything. A valve is a one-visit fix. Galvanized pipe means a partial or full repipe, and we price both so you can choose. The California Plumbing Code, based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), calls for a reducing valve when street pressure is above 80 psi.
