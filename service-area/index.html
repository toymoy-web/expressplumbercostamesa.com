Our Service Area: Costa Mesa, CA and Nearby Cities

Costa Mesa Express Plumbing works every street address in Costa Mesa, CA, plus five nearby cities. Each city has its own water utility, sewer agency and permit office, and those details change who handles a problem at the curb and what a repair involves.

Costa Mesa, ZIP codes 92626 and 92627

Costa Mesa is home base. We cover Mesa Verde, the Eastside, the Westside, College Park and the South Coast Metro area. The Costa Mesa emergency plumber page explains who owns which pipe here, the Costa Mesa Sanitary District's sewer inspection rebate and what Mesa Water's water does to fixtures.

92628 is a post office box ZIP with no street delivery. If that is your mailing ZIP, give us the street address where the plumbing problem is.

How dispatch works across the area

Calls are answered 24/7 by a person who asks for the address first. That decides the arrival estimate, since a Mesa Verde call and a Laguna Beach call are very different drives.

We tell you the estimate on the phone and update you if it changes. We do not promise arrival windows we cannot keep. For a true emergency, the dispatcher also walks you through shutting off the water or stopping fixture use while the plumber is on the way.

Distance also shapes price. Nearby calls in Costa Mesa and Fountain Valley usually carry less travel time than calls at the far edge of the area, and we say so up front instead of adding it at the door.

Permits follow the city where the work happens. Costa Mesa, Newport Beach, Fountain Valley, Westminster, Garden Grove and Laguna Beach each run their own building departments, and we file with the right one. All six inspect plumbing under the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC).

Nearby cities we serve

Newport Beach

Three water utilities and three sewer agencies serve different parts of the city. The Newport Beach page sorts out who to call, plus notes on the pumped sewers of the islands and the Peninsula.

Fountain Valley

A planned city built mostly in the 1960s, so many homes share the same aging plumbing. See the Fountain Valley page for what to check first.

Westminster

Sewer service comes from the Midway City Sanitary District, formed in 1939. The Westminster page covers postwar homes and Little Saigon businesses.

Garden Grove

A district ordinance can require a camera inspection after a sewer overflow leaves your property. The Garden Grove page explains the rule and the steps.

Laguna Beach

Hillside pressure zones and a city program that reimburses half of a sewer lateral repair, up to $1,600. Read the Laguna Beach page before you start.

What to tell the dispatcher

  • The street address, including the city. Several of our cities share street names.
  • What you see: water spraying, a slow drain, sewage, no hot water or a gas smell.
  • Whether the water is off yet, and whether you found the shutoff.
  • Access details: gate codes, steep driveways, alley entry or parking limits.
  • Whether you rent, so we know who approves and pays for the work.

If you smell gas, leave the building and call SoCalGas at 1-800-427-2200 or 911 from outside before you call a plumber.

Outside these cities?

Nearby communities just outside these six cities, such as Santa Ana Heights or Midway City, are often reachable. Ask the dispatcher.

Call anyway. If your address is outside our area, we will tell you on the phone rather than send someone who cannot arrive in a reasonable time.
